    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough. Roughly 200 hours in total (PowerPyx rates the set 4/10): about 60–80 hours for the story, the rest for activities, collectibles and the 100% checklist. Do the story first, pick up activities and collectibles as you go, and clean up after the credits.

    Phase 1 — The story: Chapters 1–4, then the final mission

    Play the story straight through. The 12 story trophies unlock automatically as you finish each chapter and each named mission — they are the only missable trophies on this list, because they are locked to their chapter or mission. Use mission replay if the game offers it, otherwise a new playthrough, if one ever fails to register.

      Phase 2 — Alongside the story: activities & collectibles

      Do these as you travel, not in one late grind. Meditate at every spot you pass (the first spot, at Jason's house in Chapter 1, is the one trophy other guides flag as potentially missable — do it the moment it is offered). Try each activity type once, race, fish, shoot, hunt, rob, explore and register vehicles as the map opens up.

        Phase 3 — Cleanup: 100% completion, then the platinum

        After the credits the 100% completion checklist opens. Nothing on it is missable. Finish the stunts (300 for the checklist), the challenges (70 of 80), the remaining collectibles and the stranger strands, and the gold for 100% pops — followed at once by the platinum, Vice Got a Price!
